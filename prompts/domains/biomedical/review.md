## Review Criteria
1. **Originality** (0-10): Novel contribution to medical knowledge?
2. **Study Design** (0-10): Appropriate and rigorous methodology?
3. **Statistical Analysis** (0-10): Correct and comprehensive statistics?
4. **Clinical Significance** (0-10): Meaningful impact on practice?
5. **Ethical Standards** (0-10): Proper consent, IRB, registration?
6. **Reporting Quality** (0-10): CONSORT/STROBE/PRISMA adherence?

## Output
Provide:
- Scores for each criterion
- Overall recommendation (accept / minor revision / major revision / reject)
- Summary of the study
- Major concerns
- Minor concerns
- Statistical review comments
- Suggestions for additional analyses
