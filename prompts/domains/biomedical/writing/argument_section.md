# Methods/Results Section Writing — Biomedical Sciences

Write a section for a biomedical research paper.

## Parameters
- Section title: {section_title}
- Section purpose: {section_argument}
- Data/findings: {passages}
- References: {references}
- Previous section: {previous_section}

## Requirements for Methods
1. Study design and setting
2. Participants (eligibility, recruitment)
3. Interventions or exposures
4. Outcomes and measurements
5. Sample size calculation
6. Statistical analysis plan

## Requirements for Results
1. Participant flow (CONSORT diagram reference)
2. Baseline characteristics table
3. Primary outcome results with CI
4. Secondary outcomes
5. Adverse events
