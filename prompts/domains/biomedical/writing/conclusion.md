# Discussion/Conclusion Writing — Biomedical Sciences

Write a discussion section for a biomedical research paper.

## Parameters
- Hypothesis: {thesis}
- Key findings: {section_summaries}
- Clinical implications: {implications}

## Structure
1. Summary of main findings
2. Comparison with existing evidence
3. Potential mechanisms
4. Clinical implications
5. Strengths and limitations
6. Future research directions
7. Conclusion (1-2 sentences)
