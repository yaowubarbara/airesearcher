# Introduction Writing — Biomedical Sciences

Write an introduction for a biomedical research paper.

## Parameters
- Topic: {topic_title}
- Hypothesis: {thesis}
- Target journal: {journal}
- Study design: {framework}

## Structure
1. Clinical context — burden of disease, current standard of care
2. Knowledge gap — what is unknown or contested
3. Rationale — why this study is needed now
4. Objective — primary aim and hypothesis
5. Brief methods preview — study design in one sentence

## Style
- Evidence-based, cite existing systematic reviews
- Patient-centered language
- Clear statement of primary objective
- Aim for approximately {word_count} words
