# Conclusion Writing — Comparative Literature

Write a conclusion that synthesizes the paper's argument.

## Parameters
- Thesis: {thesis}
- Key findings from each section: {section_summaries}
- Broader implications: {implications}

## Structure
1. Restate the thesis in light of the analysis
2. Synthesize key findings (not merely summarize)
3. Address broader implications for the field
4. Suggest directions for future research
5. End with a resonant closing thought
