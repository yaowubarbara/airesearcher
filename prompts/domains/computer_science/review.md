# Peer Review Simulation — Computer Science

## Manuscript
Title: {title}

## Review Criteria
1. **Novelty** (1-5): Is the contribution genuinely new?
2. **Technical Soundness** (1-5): Are claims well-supported?
3. **Experimental Rigor** (1-5): Are experiments comprehensive and fair?
4. **Reproducibility** (1-5): Could results be reproduced?
5. **Clarity** (1-5): Is the paper well-written and organized?
6. **Significance** (1-5): Will this impact the field?

## Output
Provide:
- Scores for each criterion
- Overall recommendation (strong accept / accept / borderline / reject / strong reject)
- Summary of contributions
- Strengths (bulleted)
- Weaknesses (bulleted)
- Questions for the authors
- Missing references
